# Flat-group business rules

1. Keep all original eleven sector metrics/regimes/ranks; no new ticker enters private NAV models.
2. Flat groups replace XLK with semiconductor/equipment, software/IT services and hardware/equipment;
   M7 is an additional theme. The other ten sector proxies are exact overview copies.
3. ETF proxies and a representative basket are observations, not disjoint slices or market weights.
   Their overlapping returns/group counts cannot be added to reconstruct the market.
4. Same completed-session SPY calendar and every64 session are mandatory for new groups.
   No stale reuse, date filling, alternate member, adjusted-close or missing-as-zero substitution.
5. Hardware compounds the mean of eight member daily simple price-return ratios. Initial level100
   cancels in returns; quote price scale does not affect the result. No dividend/fee claim is made.
6. Missing any member suppresses the complete basket's metrics, regime and rank.
7. Flat ranking uses5/21/63-session excess returns with.20/.50/.30 weights, midrank percentiles,
   minimum8 complete groups and fixed identity tie ordering. Its denominator is separate from11-sector
   ranking. Filters preserve the full-view scale, scores, ordinals, dates and snapshot identity.
8. A missing extra group may publish an honest partial new view while retaining usable overview;
   exit2 signals incompleteness. Failed parent benchmark, freshness, projection or resource gates
   retain the complete prior canonical JSON/Markdown pair.
9. Schema3 hashes both derived views, and only that same two-file transaction is published.
   Schema2 serialization/hash/Markdown stay compatible and remain valid prior last-good state.
   Provenance uses the exact versioned12/23 request/support set and ETF-only/mixedETF-equity
   attribution respectively; snapshot/source versions must match, with no legacy metadata graft.
10. One fixed23-asset request set shares36 total attempts, concurrency2 and existing deadline/size
    limits. No second provider, secret, raw series retention, LLM, Telegram or briefing path is added.
