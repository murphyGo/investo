## Resource repair

The collector retained eleven complete 10,000-point OHLCV model histories even though the
metric layer uses only the latest 64 SPY observations. This violated the intended bounded
retention in Functional Design L4.6 and caused the Ubuntu RSS failure.

Every response still passes the complete decoder and all original-row validation. Before
storing each successful result, SPY is reduced to its last 64 observations. A sector retains
its intersection with those SPY dates plus its final two IEX points, at most 66. The final two
preserve parser success/minimum history and the latest endpoint when overlap is zero or one.
No independently truncated sector tail can evict a required SPY date.

The benchmark, 11 x 10,000-row input, interpreter baseline and all resource ceilings are
unchanged. No request, retry, secret, output or publication boundary changed.
